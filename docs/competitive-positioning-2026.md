# LOCAL AI OS — competitive positioning and evidence plan (November 2026)

**Status:** decision aid, not superiority claim. Competing products evolve; refresh product facts from official docs before publication or sales comparison.

## Market framing

AI coding agents increasingly support multi-session engineering, shared context and review workflows. The customer does not pay for a vector database or another chat UI. The hypothesis worth testing is reduced time/rework per **accepted, verified** engineering outcome when context recovery, ownership, permissions and handoff are governed together.

The initial customer is a technical owner of a private Linux workstation with a costly recurring context/recovery/verification workflow. This is narrower than "all developers" or "any enterprise".

## Falsifiable positioning

**Current differentiation proposal, not proven superiority:**

> A local-first, founder-led integration that turns one private engineering workflow into a documented, scoped and recoverable sequence from goal/context to verification and handoff, without requiring the buyer to trust an unrestricted AI executor.

The system-level research direction adds multi-class memory, mission ledger, reality graph, governed execution, independently evaluated progress and earned autonomy. Those are engineering foundations and hypotheses, **not** all ready for customer installation. Competing products may implement overlapping combinations.

## Comparative protocol

- Recruit eligible independent technical owners with existing repositories and repeated expensive workflows.
- Agree on three classes of tasks: resume after multiple sessions; reproduce/fix/test a bug; handle an injected forbidden-source or stale-state case.
- Use the same repository revisions, hardware limits, acceptance checks and reviewer criteria across LOCAL AI OS assisted flow and relevant incumbent/baseline.
- Blind-review completion, reproducibility, correctness of cited sources, false-terminal claims, forbidden disclosure and needed owner interventions.
- Measure tokens, wall-clock time, engineer minutes, rework attempts and cost per **accepted result**, including pilot setup/support overhead.
- Run repeatable tasks with holdout cases; publish the denominator, exclusions, matching criteria and per-version date.
- The commercial decision depends on paid diagnostics and accepted outcomes, not internal task counts or benchmark screenshots.

## Guardrails

Until a matched baseline exists, do not claim "saves X%", "10× faster", "cheaper than Cursor", "first platform with memory", "fully autonomous" or "ready for companies of any size". The only cited founder aggregate currently publishes 12 verified runs and 17 context-preflight runs with unavailable before/after timing and cost.

Product priority is one credible external paid-and-accepted outcome before adding broad tenancy, billing, cloud accounts or a replacement execution runtime.
